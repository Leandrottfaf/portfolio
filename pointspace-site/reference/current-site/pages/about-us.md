We are the most accessible and versatile solution for geospatial data interpretation in Quebec.
Expertise
Experts in spaces and their systems, we create clear, precise, and usable plans and models by combining point clouds with cutting-edge technology. Our customized 3D solutions give our clients complete control over their infrastructures.
Mission
Our mission is to provide the most accessible and versatile solution for the interpretation of geospatial data. We use 3D scanning technologies and BIM solutions to deliver a digital twin tailored to your needs.
Meet our team
Our passionate team puts its expertise at your service to transform your ideas into concrete results. By your side every step of the way, we advise, support, and guide you toward the success of your projects.
Louis Dallaire, CEO
A graduate of ÉTS in operations and logistics engineering, Louis is passionate about the industrial world. His goal with pointSpace is to make data management and use accessible, because it's not always necessary to use complex tools to solve simple problems!
