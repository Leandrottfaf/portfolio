Founded in 2019, pointSpace is growing rapidly and looking to expand its team with passionate key partners. Competitive compensation and benefits

The role and responsibilities of the position.
In collaboration with the entire team, you will be responsible for as-built projects based on point clouds and field data.
Your experience and leadership will have an immediate impact: you will inspire the team, support daily production, and contribute to improving our standards. Your rigor, organizational skills, attention to detail, and versatility will be key to supporting the company’s growth.
Permanent full-time position – 35 to 40 hours per week
Flexible working hours between 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday
Salary: $32 to $45/hour depending on experience
Modeling from point clouds (3D scanning)
Produce QAQC drawings: plans / sections / elevations / details (layout, annotations, consistency)
Document assumptions and limitations (non-visible areas, hidden elements, survey constraints)
Participate in continuous improvement: templates, standards, methods, efficiency
Required profile
Minimum of 3 to 5 years of experience in similar roles
