Internship - Digital Marketing Project Coordinator
To make a long story short.
pointSpace is a fast-growing technology company operating in the industrial and construction sectors. We use cutting-edge tools to provide the most accessible and versatile solution for geospatial data analysis. Our mission is to help businesses optimize their operations and drive growth by offering a versatile digital twin that simplifies the management of space, machinery, and equipment.
Your mission:
As a Digital Marketing Project Coordinator at pointSpace, you will play a key role in promoting our innovative solutions and contributing to the growth of our company. You will work closely with our team and participate in diverse projects to develop our online presence, increase our visibility on social media, and support our lead generation strategy.
Are you passionate about marketing and full of ideas? This position is for you!
Required profile
Marketing skills:
Strong understanding of digital marketing concepts, data analysis, social media, and SEO.
Communication skills:
Able to communicate effectively in French and English, both written and spoken, with solid writing and presentation abilities.
Creativity:
Able to develop innovative marketing strategies and bring new ideas to the table.
Autonomy:
Able to work independently in a fast-paced start-up environment.
Tools proficiency:
Familiarity with marketing tools such as Google Ads, Google Analytics, LinkedIn, social media platforms, and creation/management tools like Canva, Duda, Pipedrive is an asset.
Passion for technology:
Strong interest in technology and innovation.
Industry knowledge:
Previous knowledge of our industry is an advantage to better understand market needs and challenges.
Due to administrative constraints, we unfortunately cannot accommodate interns residing outside of Canada at this time.
The role and responsibilities of the position.
Social media management:
Create engaging content, monitor interactions, and propose strategies to improve engagement.
Content marketing:
Produce blogs, articles, newsletters, videos, and other materials to enhance visibility and credibility while attracting new clients.
Website updates:
Maintain and update website content.
Corporate events:
Participate in the coordination and promotion of external events.
Online advertising:
Manage, supervise, and optimize Google Ads campaigns and other potential ad placements.
Lead generation strategy:
Develop email/LinkedIn campaigns, webinars, and other initiatives to generate qualified leads.
Data analysis:
Collect and analyze marketing data to measure campaign performance and identify opportunities for improvement.
Competitive intelligence:
Monitor market trends, analyze competitors, and provide insights to inform marketing strategy.
And much more!
Working in a start-up means being ready to tackle diverse tasks and contributing to the growth of a company where all ideas matter.
Why join pointSpace?
At PointSpace, you will work in a dynamic, stimulating, and learning-focused environment, alongside a team passionate about technology and innovation. Our offices, located along the Lachine Canal with downtown views, foster creativity and collaboration.
You will be surrounded by young professionals who understand market challenges and offer a unique level of autonomy to develop your skills. The schedule is flexible, your work has a real impact, and each project gives you the opportunity to grow within an innovative start-up.
