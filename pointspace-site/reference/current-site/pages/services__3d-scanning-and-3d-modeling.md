## FAQ

**Q: What types of services and deliverables do you offer?**

We provide complete 3D LiDAR scanning and modeling services for buildings, infrastructure, and industrial facilities across Quebec and Ontario. From on-site data capture to modeling, we handle every stage up to the delivery of the final product. Deliverables are tailored to your needs, projects, and workflows, ranging from point clouds to detailed 2D plans and 3D models. We deliver the final result in your preferred formats, such as Revit, CAD, STEP, SAT, and more.

**Q: What level of accuracy do you achieve with LiDAR scanning?**

We use laser sensors equipped with LiDAR technology, providing millimeter-level accuracy and ensuring high-quality, precise, and reliable deliverables.

**Q: Which regions do you serve?**

We offer our services throughout Quebec and Ontario. Data capture is performed on-site, while our team remotely manages drafting, modeling, and data processing. Deliverables are securely provided in your preferred formats, such as Revit, CAD, STEP, SAT, and more.
