Nadco Plastics
2D Layout Plans for a Manufacturing Facility
Setting the scene
Plastiques Nadco specializes in plastic injection and makes custom pieces for various clients in many industrial sectors. Its facility includes multiple production lines and a large number of machines, resulting in a dense and constantly evolving environment.
As part of its growth and optimization strategy, the company aimed to completely redesign its factory layout to integrate new equipment and improve overall operational efficiency.
However, several challenges stood in the way:
A warehouse filled with machinery, making manual measurements complex
Lack of up-to-date and reliable plans
Difficulty testing different layouts without disrupting production
In this context, pointSpace was mandated to digitize the existing environment and provide practical tools to support the reconfiguration of the facility.
This project supports
factory layout planning
,
industrial 3D scanning
, factory
drafting
and manufacturing space optimization needs, which are essential for
manufacturing companies
looking to plan their growth with reliable data.
By transforming the existing environment into usable deliverables, pointSpace helps teams
visualize constraints
, test different layout scenarios and make better decisions before integrating new equipment.
Proposed solutions
To meet Plastiques NADCO’s needs, pointSpace deployed a comprehensive approach combining data capture and modeling:
3D Laser Scanning of the Facility
Full scan of machinery and production lines performed on-site by Maxime.
Accurate capture of the environment despite the density of equipment.
2D AutoCAD Plan (Top View)
Creation of a detailed plan reflecting the current state of the facility.
A clear and structured base for layout planning.
AutoCAD Block Creation
Each machine was modeled as an individual block (entity).
Equipment can be easily moved within the plan.
Layout Simulation
Layout Simulation Tool
Interactive plan allowing the client to test different configurations
Direct support for decision-making when adding new machines
Gains
Thanks to our services, our client was able to:
Optimize its facility layout
Test multiple configurations without physically moving equipment.
Increase operational flexibility
Quickly adjust layouts by repositioning machines virtually.
Reduce risks and errors
Rely on precise data instead of manual measurements or estimates.
Accelerate growth planning
Strategically integrate new equipment with confidence and efficiency.
