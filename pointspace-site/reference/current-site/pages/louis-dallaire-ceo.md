The material differentiating pointSpace
By Louis Dallaire
•
March 19, 2026
3D scanning has become an essential tool for the fields of architecture, engineering, construction, industry, and property management. Whether it’s to reorganize your production line, optimize your facility, or much more, it enables you to capture reality with precision, improve decision-making, and reduce errors during the design and execution phases. At Technologie pointSpace, we present two of the cutting-edge Leica Geosystems 3D laser scanners we use—the Leica BLK360 and the Leica RTC360—to meet a wide range of needs. The Leica BLK360 stands out primarily for its compact size and ease of use. Designed to be highly portable, it allows for the rapid capture of indoor environments or small sites with great efficiency. Its lightweight format and intuitive operation make it an ideal solution for professionals looking to integrate 3D scanning into their workflow without technical complexity. The BLK360 produces colorized point clouds and images in a short amount of time, while ensuring automatic alignment of scans directly in the field. This approach greatly simplifies raw data processing and speeds up data delivery. It is particularly well suited for renovation projects, Scan-to-BIM modeling of interior spaces, and the documentation of existing buildings. The Leica RTC360, on the other hand, is intended for larger and more complex projects where precision, speed, and range are essential. This high-performance 3D laser scanner is capable of capturing millions of points per second, enabling the digitization of large environments with exceptional detail. Thanks to its built-in visual tracking technology, the RTC360 automatically links scans together in real time, reducing the risk of errors and the time required for data processing. Its extended range and HDR imaging quality make it an ideal solution for construction sites, industrial facilities, complex infrastructure, and projects requiring extremely precise documentation. The RTC360 integrates seamlessly with advanced BIM workflows and professional processing and modeling software. The choice between the Leica BLK360, the Leica RTC360, or another of our devices mainly depends on the nature and scale of the project. The BLK360 offers an accessible, fast, and mobile solution for small to medium-sized projects, while the RTC360 meets the demands of larger environments and projects requiring higher precision and broader coverage. In both cases, these technologies provide reliable, usable data that meets current industry standards. At pointSpace, our expertise goes beyond the use of advanced equipment. We support our clients at every stage, from data capture to data utilization, ensuring accurate results tailored to their objectives. Whether your project requires a flexible, compact solution or high-performance 3D scanning technology, we provide the equipment and expertise needed to transform reality into actionable data. To learn more, feel free to visit our social media to watch an explanatory video by Louis!
