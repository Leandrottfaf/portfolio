Setting the scene
Since 1995, St-Denis Thompson has established itself as a leader in building restoration and construction in Montreal. The company has diversified its skills, moving from traditional masonry to general contracting covering all types of building work, including the complete rehabilitation of iconic buildings. Today, with over 350 employees, St-Denis Thompson stands out for its commitment to competence, collaboration and creativity.
For a project to restore the facades of an apartment tower, they asked pointSpace to provide a complete scan of the facades to help them manage the work.
For a restoration project or facade intervention, survey accuracy is essential. Clear documentation of existing conditions helps teams better understand the actual state of the building, identify the areas that require work, coordinate the different stakeholders and monitor project progress with greater reliability.
