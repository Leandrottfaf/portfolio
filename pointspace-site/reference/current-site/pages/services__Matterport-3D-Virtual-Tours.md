1
Project setup and access planning

2
On site Matterport capture

3
Quality control and alignment

Are virtual tours secure?
Yes, we use Matterport’s data visualization platform, which provides multiple security options. You can assign one or more unique tour administrators through Matterport accounts, restrict access with password-protected links, share unsecured links, or even make tours publicly accessible. These options give you precise control over who can view your virtual tours and help protect your data.
What are virtual tours, and how do they work?
Virtual tours combine multiple 360° photos
to create an interactive experience. Users can navigate through different areas of a property or site remotely, exploring each space as if they were walking through it in person.
How can I access the virtual tour of my project?
You can access your virtual tour through several methods:
Secure link: A password-protected link for secure access.
Matterport administrators: Tour admins can log in directly to their Matterport account.
Unsecured link: For simpler, open access without passwords.
Public access: Make the tour freely available online for anyone to view.
This flexibility lets you choose the security level and accessibility that best fits your needs.
Can virtual tours be integrated into websites and presentations?
Yes! Matterport virtual tours can be seamlessly integrated into various platforms:
Websites: Embed the tour directly so visitors can explore without leaving your site.
Presentations: Add interactive links or embeds to PowerPoint or other presentation formats.
Social media: Share tour links to boost visibility and engagement.
Real estate platforms: Integrate tours into home sale or rental listings to offer immersive property experiences that attract potential buyers or tenants.
These integrations help you showcase your projects innovatively while enhancing communication and collaboration.
Can you capture hard-to-reach or restricted areas?
Yes. Our technology allows us to safely capture spaces that are difficult to access or restricted, providing a complete view of your property or site without compromising safety.
