Summary

pointSpace already offers the right services and uses most of the right vocabulary. The
biggest gains are clearer French labels, headings that name the deliverable, and checking
Québec field terms against real search data before choosing final keywords.

    Field vocabulary (section 3) lists candidate expressions suggested during this
    research, not terms collected from clients or staff. Each one needs confirmation by
    Louis and the team, then a volume check in Keyword Planner.
    Core service vocabulary (section 4) is ranked by service fit and buying intent. The
    priorities are editorial, not measured search volume.
    Competitor coverage (appendix) shows how many of 8 sampled competitor pages use
    each term. It reflects standard industry wording, not what buyers type.

Not yet verified: Google Maps and organic rankings by city, and competitor traffic. Direct
Google checks were blocked during the research, so these are listed as next steps for
Thierry.

"superficie locative" or "espaces locatifs". In Montréal, most BOMA providers found are
land surveyors (arpenteurs-géomètres), who sell a "certificat de mesurage".
